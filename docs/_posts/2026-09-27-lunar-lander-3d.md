Remember the old [Lunar Lander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_Lander_(video_game_genre)), from the late 70's/early 80's? Well, this version
takes a lot of inspiration from that game, but adds a 3D twist.

Your view is the same, 2D side-on. except you fly through 3D terrain and use rotate the 2D view so you see a _slice_ of the 3D world.
There are progression levels, so to start with you get a 3D overlap to help you orient yourself and learn how to pilot the ship.

![Lunar Lander 3D](/assets/img/lunar-lander-screenshot.png)

It's not easy, but it's rather fun.
